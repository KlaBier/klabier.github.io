It was great to be part of the Security Ninja event at Microsoft in Munich.

In my session, I explored how AI can help us work with Microsoft Entra ID, looking at three different approaches:

**Security Copilot**<br>
How AI can support security investigations and help us better understand what's happening in our Entra ID environment.

**Agent Builder**<br>
Building custom AI agents to provide guidance and answer questions based on specific knowledge and content.

**MCP Server for Enterprise**<br>
Connecting AI tools to Entra ID and exploring how natural language can be used to interact with tenant configurations and identity data.

Beyond the technical capabilities, I also discussed the limitations of these approaches and where human expertise is still essential.

The many questions and discussions during and after my session showed just how much interest there is in this topic. It was great to exchange ideas, share practical experiences, and hear different perspectives.

Many thanks to the organizer team of Security Ninjas for organizing the event and to everyone who joined my session!
