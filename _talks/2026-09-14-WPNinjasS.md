It was great to be back at the [Workplace Ninjas Summit](https://summit.wpninjas.global/), this time in Baden, Switzerland.

I had the opportunity to deliver two sessions:

**Deletion Happens – Are You Ready for Recovery in Entra ID?**
A deep dive into deletion and recovery in Entra ID — what can be restored, what cannot, and how Unified Tenant Configuration Management (UTCM) changes the way we think about protecting tenant configuration.
[Session slides](/MySlides/2026-09-14-WPNinjasS_DeletionHappens.pdf)

**The Hidden Risks in Your Entra Tenant – A Housekeeping Guide**
A practical look at the forgotten and abandoned objects that accumulate in Entra ID over time, and how to identify and clean them up using logs, PowerShell, and Security Copilot.
[Session slides](/MySlides/2026-09-14-WPNinjasS_DeletionHappens_Hausekeeping.pdf)

Two very different sessions, but both with the same goal: understanding what is really happening in an Entra ID tenant and reducing unnecessary risk.
Thanks to the Workplace Ninjas community and everyone who joined my sessions. It was great to meet so many people and have some really interesting discussions!
